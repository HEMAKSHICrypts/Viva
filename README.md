# ✚ Viva – A Ray of Hope in the Darkest Hours

**Bringing hope, saving lives, when every second matters.**

Viva is an accident emergency-response platform designed to connect bystanders, police, ambulance crews, and nearby hospitals through a simple, coordinated reporting experience.

**See an accident? Snap. Send. Save.**

# The Problem

During road accidents and emergencies, delays in communication and difficulty reaching the right emergency services can cost valuable time. Bystanders may not know whom to contact or which hospital is nearby.

Viva aims to simplify the first step towards getting help.

# Our Solution

Viva provides a connected interface where users can report an accident, share their location, and initiate an emergency-response workflow.

# Key Features

- **One-Tap SOS:** Quickly initiate an emergency alert.
- **Accident Photo Reporting:** Capture or upload an accident image.
- **GPS Location:** Share the user's current location.
- **Photo Verification:** Basic image checks and an optional AI-based accident-scene check.
- **Hospital Discovery:** Identify nearby hospitals based on location.
- **Emergency Dispatch Dashboard:** View ambulance assignment and estimated arrival information in the prototype.
- **Hospital Dashboard:** View incoming alerts and hospital-related information.
- **Emergency Shortcuts:** Quick access to SOS, including shake and voice-trigger concepts.
- **Multilingual Interface:** English and Hindi language support.
- **Emergency Helpline:** Quick access to India's emergency number, 112.
- **No Login Required:** Access the reporting interface without creating an account.

# How Viva Works

1. A bystander opens Viva after witnessing an accident.
2. They trigger SOS or submit an accident photo.
3. The platform checks the image and requests location access.
4. The incident moves through a verification workflow.
5. Nearby hospitals and ambulance information are displayed in the dispatch flow.
6. The dashboards provide a view of the incident and response status.

# Important Disclaimer

Viva is currently a prototype demonstrating an emergency-response workflow. Hospital notifications, police verification, ambulance dispatch, and estimated arrival information shown in the demo are not proof of live integration with actual emergency services.

Do not rely on this prototype for real emergencies. Contact the official emergency number **112** in India.
